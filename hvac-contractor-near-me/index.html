Work, timing, and cost factors

The work may be as simple as discussing a maintenance need or may require a closer look at heating or cooling equipment. Ask the contractor to describe the proposed steps, what is included, and whether any further work might be recommended after the issue is assessed. Typical timing depends on the type of service, the condition of the equipment, and whether parts or additional work are involved.

Cost generally depends on the task, the parts or equipment required, the time involved, and how accessible the equipment is. These are factors to ask about, not a set price. Before approving work, make sure you understand what the stated scope covers and how the provider will communicate if the scope changes.

A few terms you may hear:
- HVAC: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning.
- Maintenance: Routine attention intended to keep equipment in working order. Ask what tasks are included.
- Airflow: The movement of air through equipment and the spaces it serves.
