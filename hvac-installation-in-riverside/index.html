Scope, key facts, and terms you may hear

The installation process commonly involves reviewing the existing setup, selecting equipment suited to the property, completing the agreed installation, and checking operation. Ask the contractor to spell out the exact steps for your project. These general facts are useful when you request a plan:

  • What it covers: Heating, air conditioning, or both, along with any related work listed in the proposal.
  • Typical timing: Timing depends on the equipment, the existing setup, access to the work area, and the scope. Ask for a project-specific schedule.
  • What affects cost: Equipment choice, the work needed to connect it, changes to existing components, and site conditions can all affect the total. Request a written scope and cost breakdown rather than relying on a general estimate.
  • What to clarify: Ask who handles required preparation, what happens to existing equipment, and what checks are part of the completed work.

A few terms are worth knowing. Capacity describes how much heating or cooling equipment can provide. Ductwork is the network that carries conditioned air through a building. Commissioning refers to checks made to confirm equipment is set up and operating as intended. Ask the contractor to explain any term that appears in your proposal.
